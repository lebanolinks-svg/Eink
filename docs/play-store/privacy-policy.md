# InkPhone privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

InkPhone is an Android app that gives a phone a calmer, e-ink style look and helps limit screen time.

## The short version

InkPhone does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

## What the app reads on your phone, and why

Everything below is read and used only on your device.

| Information | Why InkPhone uses it | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| The list of apps you can open, with their names and icons | To show your apps on the InkPhone home screen and redraw their icons | Stays on your phone |
| App usage time (Android "usage access", only if you allow it) | To show today's screen time and most used apps, to enforce the daily app limits you set, and to know which app is in front so the paper wash can step aside | Stays on your phone |
| Your InkPhone settings (pinned and hidden apps, limits, home screen layout) | To remember your choices | Stored in the app's private storage on your phone |

## Permissions

- **Usage access**: screen time, most used apps and app limits. Optional.
- **Display over other apps**: the optional paper wash and the "time's up" page shown over an app whose limit has run out. Optional.
- **Foreground service**: keeps the limits and the paper wash running while you use other apps. A notification is shown while it runs.
- **Run at startup**: restarts that service after the phone restarts, if you had it on.
- **Modify secure settings**: only works if you grant it yourself from a computer. It lets InkPhone switch Android's greyscale and animation settings for you.

InkPhone does not use an accessibility service and does not read the content of your screen, messages, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

## Widgets from other apps

If you place another app's widget on the InkPhone home screen, that widget is run by its own app under that app's privacy policy. InkPhone only displays it.

## Children

InkPhone is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
